|---|---|

Generated by `../scripts/build_fixtures.py` (byte-exact, rebuildable). `regions.json` records the
raw byte ranges `[byte_start, byte_end)` of the dt...dd runs the author intends as units;
`expectations.json` holds the cases, expected boundaries and reasons; `expectations_resolved.json`
is written by `../scripts/check_expectations.py` from the bytes alone. All three were committed
before the selection helper and its tests existed.

Byte ranges here are ranges of the retained HTML files. They are never parser line numbers,
offsets into a DOM serialization, or offsets into extracted text.
